A newspaper front page that sums up the world news of Tuesday 24 November 2026 in one image.

`draw.py` draws the page with PIL: masthead "THE WORLD" with the date big and clear, the Nvidia comeback as the lead story (Jensen Huang reinstated, shares up 31.5%), ten story cards, a 30-story "day in brief" index, and a by-the-numbers strip — 41 verified stories in all, each with its source. `main.py` copies the finished `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` and validates it (PNG, under 1 MiB, 1024x1024).

Every story on the page happened or was first reported on 24 November 2026.
